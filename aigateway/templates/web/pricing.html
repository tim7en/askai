Yes. Pay-as-you-go supports credit card payments including UZS-denominated cards. Enterprise plans support direct invoicing in UZS.

Requests exceeding your rate limit will receive a 429 status code. You can retry after a short wait. Upgrade your plan for higher limits.

Yes. Pay-as-you-go and Enterprise plans support monthly budgets with optional hard caps that stop requests when the limit is reached.

Our router selects the best model and provider based on your task type, cost preferences, and available credentials. You can also pin a specific provider or model.

The gateway adds minimal overhead (typically <50ms). Requests are proxied directly to providers. Streaming is fully supported.

We log metadata (token counts, costs, latency) but do not store prompt or completion content by default. Enterprise plans can configure data retention policies.
